prints $\alpha^{-1}_{\mathrm{tree}} = 4\pi\gr^4/\Hbfour$, which evaluates

exact tree-level identity, now suite-pinned:
$\alpha^{-1}_{\mathrm{tree}}\,\gs = 9\pi\ln2/10 = 1.95983$.
\end{mdframed}

\begin{mdframed}[backgroundcolor=Ebg,linecolor=Ecolor,linewidth=1.2pt]
\textbf{TN1-2 \SUP\ --- the dual-route strain, demoted.} TN-1's
Route~1, $\gs_{K_4} = 3/8 - \Hbfour$, its Proposition ($K_4$--Holographic
Strain: ``holographic capacity cannot quite satisfy gauge unification''),
